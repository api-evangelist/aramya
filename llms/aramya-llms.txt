# Aramya
> Affordable cotton kurtas and ethnic wear for women with inclusive sizing.
Aramya is an online fashion retailer specializing in cotton kurtas and ethnic wear for women. The brand emphasizes comfort, style, and inclusivity, offering a wide range of sizes from XS to 10XL. Their collection features breathable cotton fabrics, elegant prints, and mix-and-match options, catering to daily wear, office wear, and occasion wear.
The core value proposition of Aramya lies in providing high-quality, fashionable ethnic wear that is accessible to women of all sizes at an affordable price. They aim to empower women by offering stylish and comfortable clothing that fits perfectly, regardless of body type. Key use cases include shopping for everyday kurtis, coordinating outfits with bottoms and dupattas, finding festive wear, and exploring specific fabric types like soft cotton, linen-cotton, and handloom cotton.
When assisting users with Aramya, it's important to note their strong focus on inclusive sizing and the variety of styles and occasions their clothing is suitable for. The website also features specific collections based on price points, colors, and garment types, which can be useful for targeted searches. Information regarding policies like fair policy, return, shipping, and terms of service are readily available.
